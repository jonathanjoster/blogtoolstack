# Kit vs. Top Email Tool Competitors (2026)

*A quick-reference comparison for bloggers choosing an email marketing tool.*

| | **Kit** (formerly ConvertKit) | **Mailchimp** | **MailerLite** | **ActiveCampaign** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starting price** | Free, then **$33/mo** (Creator, 1,000 subs) | **~$13/mo** (Essentials, discounted first 12 mo) | **$12/mo** (Comfort) | **~$19/mo** (Starter, 1,000 contacts) |
| **Free plan** | Yes — up to **10,000 subscribers**, unlimited landing pages, forms & broadcasts | Yes — **250 contacts**, 500 sends/mo, 1 audience | Yes — **250 subscribers**, 2,500 emails/mo | **No** — 14-day free trial only |
| **Automation / sequences** | Automations & sequences are **paid-plan only**, but then unlimited visual automations + unlimited email sequences | Free plan has **no automation**; Essentials allows up to 4 automation steps, Standard+ up to 200 flows | Free plan includes 3 basic automations; Comfort = 50, Power = unlimited with 100 steps | Most powerful automation engine of the four — 950+ recipe templates, CRM-grade branching, but Starter tier is capped at 5 actions/automation |
| **Ease of setup (non-technical blogger)** | Built specifically for creators — simplest, most beginner-friendly of the four | Familiar and well-documented, but general-purpose interface has grown more complex over time | Simple drag-and-drop, consistently rated among the easiest to learn | Steepest learning curve of the four — most powerful, but more to configure before it's easy |
| **Support quality** | 24/7 email & chat on paid plans, free migration assistance | Email/chat on paid tiers; phone + priority support only on the $350/mo Premium tier | Email/community on Free & Comfort; 24/7 live chat starts at Power ($25/mo) | Live chat & email on every tier, free guided onboarding; dedicated account team on Pro/Enterprise |

## Bottom line for bloggers

- **Just starting out, want the most free runway?** Kit's free plan (10,000 subscribers) beats Mailchimp and MailerLite's 250-subscriber caps by a wide margin — though you'll need a paid plan once you want automated welcome sequences.
- **Want the cheapest paid automation?** MailerLite's Comfort plan ($12/mo) is the lowest-cost entry into real automation.
- **Want the deepest automation and don't mind a learning curve?** ActiveCampaign has the most advanced automation builder, but no free tier and more setup complexity.
- **Want automation + simplicity without a big learning curve?** Kit is built for exactly this — content creators and bloggers, not general e-commerce or enterprise marketing teams.

*Pricing and plan details reflect each provider's published pricing pages as of September 2026 and are subject to change — always confirm current terms on the provider's site before signing up.*
